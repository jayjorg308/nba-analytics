# Player comparisons include free throws; split comparisons defer them

The comparison page's free-throw section ships in Players mode only. The prototype plan deferred free throws because ADR-0073 requires every included section to consume the exact comparison windows, and the free-throw contract is season-total shaped — but in Players mode the two windows ARE full seasons, so the season-total contract matches the windows exactly and nothing is silently substituted. The section loads both sides' deployed free-throw payloads (the fourth sibling, ADR-0053), asserts their league free-throw lines identical before sharing one ruler (ADR-0074's stance: a mismatch is a contradiction, never a silent choice), aggregates each side through the one free-throw aggregation (ADR-0055 semantics: technicals included on both sides for endpoint parity), and pairs the trip taxonomy by class identity. It renders in the ADR-0078 scoreboard grammar (the C variant of a four-variant throwaway prototype against the shipping side-by-side tables — current, transpose-only, season cards + transpose, full per-class cards): three season-line cards lead, each with one call over a mini table of both sides, the Lg ruler row, and the raw counts each value rounds from — the calls live at the season line because that is where full-season sample sizes support them — and the trip taxonomy follows transposed to a row per side, the class name spanning both rows, so each name sits beside its own numbers. The call vocabulary extends ADR-0078's asymmetry: drawing fouls (Draw edge, FTA rate) and converting (Conversion edge, FT%) are results against the league, while FT share of points only leans (Reliance lean) because a scoring mix has no better direction. Per-class calls were rejected with the D variant: most classes sit under the 50-FTA bar, and a wall of flagged chips is noise, not answers. Every call decides and prices on the gap of its two displayed anchors (ADR-0023), inheriting † from either side (ADR-0075's rule at the line), and no surface grades the comparison as a whole. Split mode keeps free throws deferred, for the three reasons the contract cannot window by date: trips carry no gameDate, technical free throws are season scalars that cannot be apportioned to a window, and the FTA-rate denominator is the pre-drop season FGA, which does not window exactly (zoneConflictsDropped is season-level). A date-grained free-throw contract is the prerequisite for the split-mode section, not a rendering change.
