# A player's season is a tool page with season permalinks

Every player with a shot for a team in a season has a season page on that team's surface (`src/app/PlayerSeasonPage.tsx`, `src/domain/playerSeason.ts`; decisions 10 to 13 and 18 of `docs/plans/jazz-home-transition.md`). `/jazz/<player-slug>` is his canonical alias, rendering the team's canonical season in place, and `/jazz/<season>/<player-slug>` is the permalink of any registered season: ADR-0060's pattern at team grain, so a 2025-26 report card's names link 2025-26 pages and a player who left keeps a valid one. The page is the tool class (ADR-0081, ADR-0075): no verdict, no thesis, no reading of the numbers, honesty carried by counts and local flags. **Amends ADR-0060 and ADR-0088.**

**What it shows.** The poster banner (ADR-0094) in a third form, the headshot: the type poster with his standard NBA headshot (ADR-0065's asset) standing at its right edge, in the site's black and white, his jersey number from the season's roster snapshot in the kicker. Under it the frontier byline (his games, his shots), the way back to the team season, his prior season's page when he shot for the team in it, and his argument when he has one. Then the season pair (the hero page's headline cards, his diet and conversion against the season's frontier ruler) and three numbered acts: 01 · THE GAMES, his quadrant, one dot per game with a priced shot, each against that game's own pre-game ruler (ADR-0089), his latest game highlighted and his prior season's games faded behind; 02 · THE SEASON, zone by zone against the season's league average with the comparison page's local flags; 03 · THE LOG, every game he appeared in, newest first, his box line beside his priced shots, each row linking its report card. A game's numbers are the same function over the same rows as his line on that game's card, and the deployed guard (`playerSeason.real.test.ts`) holds every player of every published season to it.

**Who has a page, and his slug.** Every shooter of the season, rostered or departed, by one mechanical rule: a player with free throws but no shot all season has nothing to price and no page. A slug is his name lowercased, diacritics folded, punctuation dropped, words hyphenated (`playerSlug`), so "Moussa Cissé" and the feed's 2026-10-27 "Cisse" share `moussa-cisse`, and "Terrence Shannon Jr." survives its rename to "Jr". Hero slugs follow the same rule, so one headshot file serves a hero's directory figure and his tool page. Two shooters sharing a slug in one season is a contract failure, never an overwrite. The route grammar never collides: a player slug starts with a letter, a season or game slug with a digit; the third segment after a game slug is reserved for the player's night.

**The game log reads the season index, which now carries player lines.** A season of games must be one small file, never every game file (82 of them, about 10 MB). So the season index (ADR-0088) moves to schema v2: each game row carries the box line of every subject player who appeared, the game file's own line less the technical and split free-throw counts only a receipt itemizes. The load boundary re-asserts that the players sum to the box team line on the game payload's own fields (never assists, which reconcile against assist statuses, ADR-0046), and the deployed guard holds every row's lines equal to its game file's. The 2025-26 index was re-exported through the scratch store and all 82 game files came out unchanged; the 2026-27 index through a read-only export of the production store, its five game files identical in content to the published ones. The 2025-26 index grows from 150 KB to 397 KB (30 KB compressed).

**Arguments and tool pages link both ways.** The player page links his argument: the season argument when one exists for the page's season, his canonical one otherwise. A season argument whose player shot for a team that season names it (`teamPage` in the hero config) and gains "Game by game", beside ADR-0060's forward link on the same line. That is a second structural link on a frozen argument; the verdict and its guard file do not change. `src/teams/playerPages.real.test.ts` holds the field both ways: set exactly when the player has a page for that team and season. A future Jazz argument (Peterson's at his flip) therefore fails the suite until it names its team page.

**Headshots are a mechanical asset.** `npm run team:headshots` fetches the standard headshot for every player with a page who lacks one, with hero:add's downloader (ADR-0066), into the conventional `img/<slug>-headshot.png`; the first run added 27, about 5 MB, beside the three heroes' already committed. A missing file hides the image and the banner reads as the type poster, never a placeholder.

**Consequences.** The report card's player names link their season pages. Player pages have no emitted share page yet (a shared link previews as the site's card); that waits for the home's share work. A future contributor must not compute a player's game numbers any way but his slice under that game's ruler, must not read a season of game files where the index serves, and must not give a page to a player without a shot.

**Amendment (2026-10-29, share pages):** the share-page consequence above lifts. The build emits a page for every shooter of every registered season (`scripts/emit-hero-pages.ts`, `playerPageMeta` in `src/teams/shareCards.ts`): `dist/jazz/<player-slug>/index.html` for the canonical season and `dist/jazz/<season>/<player-slug>/index.html` for the others. Each carries the page's runtime title (`<name> · <season> · Utah Jazz`), its own `og:url`, and preloads of the files the page fetches (the season's index and payload, and the prior season's when that season is registered). The image is the card of his season's own page (ADR-0087), never a card of his own. A per-player card in the hero card's marquee grammar was considered and declined: the season loop would have to draw one for every new shooter, from a headshot that today lands only by PR (`team:headshots`), or the build, part of the loop's gate, would fail on his debut. The season card is committed before his first shot, so a debut cannot fail the build. A canonical-season permalink (`/jazz/<canonical season>/<player-slug>`) gets no page, as the canonical season page is emitted at `/jazz` alone, and falls back to the root's. A registered season with no deployed team payload fails the build, since the shooters are read from it.

**Amendment (2026-10-31, the player's night, ADR-0096):** the third segment after a game slug is now the player's night (`/jazz/<game-slug>/<player-slug>`). His game-log rows and his quadrant's dots open his nights instead of the report cards, and the report card's player names open nights instead of season pages; the night links up to his season page and across to the card, so both stay one click away.
