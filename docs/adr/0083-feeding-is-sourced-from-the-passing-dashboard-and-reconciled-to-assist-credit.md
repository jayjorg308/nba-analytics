# Feeding is sourced from the passing dashboard and reconciled to assist credit

**Feeding**, who passed to a player before his shots including the misses, ships as the **feeding payload** (`public/data/_teams/<tricode>/<season>.feeding.json`), a team-scoped sixth contract sourced from the NBA tracking **passing dashboard** (`playerdashptpass`, the passes-received frame) pulled per rostered player and anchored to the same frontier as the team shot payload. Per receiver, each feeder row carries the source's counts verbatim: passes, assists, attempts and makes, with the two- and three-point splits. Nothing is inferred from play-by-play sequence, from shot proximity, or from any Case 1 field (ADR-0005 stands).

**The oracle.** For every receiver, the sum of assists across his feeders equals the count of his made shots carrying explicit official assist credit in the play-by-play through the frontier, parsed by the ADR-0041 grammar and already reconciled per team per game to the box score (ADR-0046). A tracking sum **below** the play-by-play count is a tracking gap, persisted as `assistShortfall` and reported whenever nonzero, the ADR-0030 shortfall pattern; a tracking sum **above** it is a contradiction and halts. Feeder attempts never exceed the receiver's row count in the team shot payload. The oracle reconciles what both sources observe (assisted makes); the miss-inclusive counts are the tracking dashboard's own observation and have no second source, which is why they ship descriptive.

**Descriptive, never league-relative.** No league feeding baseline exists at this grain and none is computed (a league average of pass counts would be a rate average over unequal rosters, ADR-0004's forbidden operation). Feeding therefore follows ADR-0038: counts with denominators, no ability claim, no ranking. A feeding row is a **pass**; the word **assist** is reserved for scorer credit on a make, and copy never calls a pass that produced a miss an assist.

**Consequences.** The season loop's team session adds roughly one call per rostered player per night, anchored with `DateTo`. The feeding payload copies the team shot payload's frontier fields and the deployed-pair guard asserts their equality. A future contributor must not fill a shortfall, must not compute a league feeding average, and must not derive a per-shot passer from play-by-play prose or timing.
