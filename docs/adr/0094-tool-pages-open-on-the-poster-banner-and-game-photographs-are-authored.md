# Tool pages open on the poster banner, and a game's photograph is authored

The hero banner (ADR-0021, ADR-0025) becomes one shared component, `src/app/PosterBanner.tsx`, which every page that leads with a banner renders. Hero pages moved onto it with their markup byte-identical. The team report card is the first tool page on it, replacing the compact tool header the team surface opened with (ADR-0081); the Jazz home and the player pages of `docs/plans/jazz-home-transition.md` follow. A tool page still carries no thesis: the report card's h1 is the score over the night's title, which is computed copy (ADR-0090), and the cue reads "↓ The card". **Amends ADR-0081 and ADR-0091.**

**Two forms of art, never a borrowed one.** A photograph keeps the hero treatment exactly: grayscale, a right-anchored panel in the wide layout, and on a portrait handheld the full first viewport. The typographic form is the banner with no photograph: content height in every layout, so a phone never opens on a screen of black and the first act follows at once (about 300px tall at 375px wide), with the team mark ghosted where the photo would sit. A game publishes at 23:45 with no person involved, so every game opens typographic, and a photograph arrives later, by PR, if it arrives at all. A photograph from another game or season never stands in: its alt text would describe a different night.

**A game's photograph is authored config, never payload.** The game file stays immutable data (ADR-0088). Photographs live in `src/teams/gamePhotos.ts`, keyed by game slug, each entry a hero banner's shape: the committed web-sized derivative, its alt text (VOICE.md's banner rules: the frame only), its credit in the two house forms, and both focal points. `npm run game:photo -- <game-slug> <image>` writes the derivative (EXIF applied then dropped, the longest side capped at 2048px, progressive JPEG) and stubs the entry with `TODO(scaffold)` sentinels. The guard, `src/teams/gamePhotos.test.ts` over `gamePhotoAuthoring.ts`, holds the suite red until every field is authored, the image is on disk, the game is published, the alt text and credit keep the punctuation rule and the credit keeps a house form, the focal points read "x% y%", and the game's share card has been redrawn with this photograph. It is ADR-0063's tripwire at game grain, and its synthetic cases prove each check fires, so an empty config is never a vacuous pass.

**The credit rides with the photograph.** A game photograph is credited on its own card: a small line under the banner, and the bottom-right corner of its share card. The methodology page's SOURCES section keeps hero banners and gains one sentence saying game photographs are credited on their cards, so the list does not grow all season. `docs/image-credits.md` keeps the engineering provenance, as for every image. A photograph with no known credit renders "photograph credit pending", ADR-0071's rule.

**A photo PR redraws exactly one share card.** ADR-0091 lets the season loop add cards and never redraw one. A game whose photograph lands is redrawn once, by a person, in the photo PR (`npm run cards:games -- <game-slug>`): the photograph takes the court's place as the banner's wide panel, the credit sits in the corner, and the PNG carries a text chunk naming the photograph (`goodshots:photo`), which is how the guard proves the redraw happened. A card with no photograph is drawn exactly as before, with no chunk: redrawing photo-less cards after this change produced byte-identical files, so the loop's cards never change. The generator refuses an entry still carrying sentinels. Link previews already cached by a platform may keep the earlier card; the site serves the new one.

**The body under the banner was chosen by a prototype (2026-10-29).** Three variants on the existing route (`?variant=`, the house method): A, the banner over the existing card; B, the argument's rhythm (the score alone in the banner, the two answers as a verdict-sized line, the hero page's headline cards, numbered acts stacked); C, every act a visual and its data twin. The author chose C without its per-player expected-versus-scored chart, which only repeated the players table's columns. The card now reads in numbered acts, the hero page's recipe (ADR-0051): 01 · THE NIGHT (the quadrant with the two answers beside it), 02 · THE FLOORS (both teams' courts side by side over one zone-count table with a column per team), 03 · THE TERMS (both sides on the same terms), 04 · THE PLAYERS. B was declined because answering in a verdict-sized line made a tool page read as an argument; the opponent's court in C is information the card did not show before.

**Consequences.** A page that adds a banner uses `PosterBanner`, never a copy of the hero markup. The Jazz home's season banner and the player pages' headshot banner are later forms of the same component, each added with its page. A future contributor must not put a photograph in a game payload, must not let the loop draw or redraw a photo card, must not show one game's photograph on another game's card, and must not loosen the photo guard to admit an unauthored entry.
