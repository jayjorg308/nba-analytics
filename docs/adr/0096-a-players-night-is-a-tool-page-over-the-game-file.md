# A player's night is a tool page over the game file

Every appearance a team player makes in a published game has a night on the team surface: `/jazz/<game-slug>/<player-slug>` (`src/app/PlayersNightPage.tsx`, `src/domain/playersNight.ts`; Stage 4 of `docs/plans/jazz-home-transition.md`, decided 2026-10-30). It is one player's game as priced facts, read from the game file the report card already reads (ADR-0088) and priced against that game's own pre-game ruler (ADR-0089), so its two numbers are his dot on his season page and his line on the game's report card. The tool class (ADR-0075, ADR-0081): structural copy, computed copy, and counts, never a verdict. It replaces the game cards (`/game`, ADR-0086), which retire in a follow-up PR. **Amends ADR-0086 and ADR-0095.**

**Who has a night.** Every subject player with a box line in the game, his appearances without an attempt included (in 2025-26, 14 of 813 appearances had no field-goal attempt and 11 no attempt at all). A night with no attempt shows his box line and a structural no-attempt line; a night of free throws or heaves alone has a receipt but no answers. An opponent's night is not a page, though the file carries both teams: the surface is the team's. The night resolves his slug against the game's subject box with the season page's rule (`playerSlug`), so a player with free throws but no shot all season has nights and no season page, and a slug outside the box gets a plain note linking his season page when he has one. Two box lines sharing a slug in one game is a contract failure.

**What it shows.** Chosen 2026-10-31 from a two-variant prototype (B, Composite, over A, the report card's numbered acts). The poster banner in its headshot form (ADR-0094, ADR-0095), every night, since a game photograph is the report card's and may not show him. Under it the byline, the result and his box line, linking up to his season page and across to the game's report card. Then one poster block: the two answers at player grain, **Good shots?** and **Did they fall?**, worded by the report card's computed-copy templates (ADR-0090) on the displayed gap, with his heaves and zone-point conflicts counted beside them; THE LINE and THE CREDIT as chips; his court; and the **receipt** behind a drill-in, every attempt and trip in game order (period, the clock running down, a make before the free throws it drew) down to his box points, heaves and conflicts struck, technical and split free throws on their own lines, the priced attempts' expected and scored points as its subtotal. Last, his season so far on the game quadrant, this game ringed, his prior season faded behind, every dot opening his night in that game; and the ruler in one line.

**Free throws stay unpriced.** The ruler carries zone field-goal rates only, so THE LINE reads as counts (trips and their points by class, the free-throw line, FTA rate, technicals) and the receipt's trip rows carry no expected value, as on the report card. Pricing trips at a league free-throw rate was declined: it needs a new pull per ruler date and a reviewed rewrite of every immutable game file. This is the game card's one priced feature the night drops.

**A night equals his season page.** `buildPlayersNight` throws unless his two deltas equal his dot's and his expected and scored points equal his game-log row's, the report card's stance at team grain; `src/domain/playersNight.real.test.ts` builds every appearance of every published season, holds every game-log row's link to a night that resolves under his season page's slug, and every report card player line to its night. The quadrant stops at the game, so a shared night never moves (the report card's rule).

**Links.** The report card's PLAYERS names open nights (ADR-0095 linked them to season pages); his season page's game-log rows and quadrant dots open his nights, and the quadrant's hint says so ("Click to open his night"). The night links up to his season page and across to the card, which keeps the card one click from the season page. No prev and next links: the quadrant's dots open his other nights.

**Share pages, over the game's card.** The build emits `dist/jazz/<game-slug>/<player-slug>/index.html` for every subject player of every registered season's index rows (`nightPageMeta`), with the page's runtime title (`<name> · <date> · Utah Jazz`), its own `og:url`, and preloads of what the report card fetches, the same files the night fetches. The image is the game's committed card. A card per night was declined: about 90 MB of committed PNGs a season, drawn by the loop every game night.

**No new data.** The night reads the game file, the season index, and the team shot payload (and the prior season's two for the faded dots). Nothing is pulled, loaded, or exported for it.

**Consequences.** The third route segment after a game slug, reserved by ADR-0095, is the night. The game cards and their contract retire in a follow-up PR (decision 9): the card page and landing, the game-log payload files, `export_gamelog_payload.py`, `regen_gamelog_golden.py`, the card-roster import and `CARD_ROSTER_MIN_FGA`, the golden pair, and the deployed guard, with the three Jazz heroes' card URLs forwarded to their nights and the `/game` landing to the Jazz home. A future contributor must not price a night any way but his slice under the game's own ruler, must not give an opponent a night, and must not price a trip without a ruler that carries free-throw rates.
