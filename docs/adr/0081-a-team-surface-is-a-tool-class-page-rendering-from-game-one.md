# A team surface is a tool-class page that renders from game one

The Utah Jazz team surface (`/jazz`, the Jazz surface plan) is a **tool**, the class ADR-0075 opened for the comparison page, not an **argument**, the class every hero page belongs to. It carries structural copy only: no verdict, no per-game note, no whole-team claim, and therefore no verdict guards. Its honesty is carried locally by visible counts and the existing sample flags (a zone under 15 attempts is too thin for a stable selection reading; making under 50 attempts keeps the uncertainty flag), never by suppression. It renders from the first game of the season with whatever the reconciled frontier holds.

**Why the hero gates do not apply.** ADR-0003's gates and ADR-0059's dark period exist to protect a *published argument* from thin data: a verdict written over eight games is the worst page the product can ship. A team page makes no argument, so there is nothing to protect by hiding it, and a reader who sees "1 game, 84 shots" beside every number has been told exactly what the numbers are worth. Hiding a tool until it is statistically comfortable would also contradict the parking lot's own answer for rookies pre-flip: counts with plain denominators are the honest pre-gate surface, and the team page is where they live.

**What does apply, unchanged.** The frontier rule (ADR-0058): the surface publishes exactly through a reconciled frontier or not at all, lag defers, contradiction halts, no tolerances. The data-only automated commit class (ADR-0057). Presentation never computes (ADR-0011); every number on the page comes from the unchanged aggregation function over an exact slice of a deployed payload. Displayed gaps subtract displayed anchors (ADR-0023). Zones are data-assigned (ADR-0012). The app reads committed JSON only (ADR-0080).

**Consequences.** The surface's copy is checked the way structural copy is checked, against the code and the deployed payloads, and a copy guard forbids verdict, grading, and claim vocabulary on it. Hero pages for Jazz players stay complete arguments at their own URLs and are linked from the surface, never duplicated or summarized. A future contributor must not add an authored team verdict, a "state of the offense" note, or any graded reading to this surface without a new decision and its own claim kind; must not hide the page or a section behind a maturity bar; and must not let a per-game figure render without its attempt count beside it.
