# The site root is the Jazz home, and the directory moves to /arguments

Good Shots opens on the Utah Jazz (decisions 1, 7 to 9, 14, and 15 of `docs/plans/jazz-home-transition.md`). `/` renders the home team's canonical season, the Jazz home; `/jazz` renders the same page in place, an alias, never a redirect; `/jazz/<season>` renders any registered season in the same layout. The directory of arguments moves from the root to `/arguments`, a reserved route like `/methodology`, with the same marquee and rail and the Jazz arguments first. Every hero URL stays where it was. **Amends ADR-0022 and ADR-0065**, and retires ADR-0084's deployed ledger file.

**The page.** A tool (ADR-0081), chosen 2026-10-29 from a two-variant prototype on the five published 2026-27 games: A, Acts, over B, Front page. A is one column of numbered acts in the report card's recipe (ADR-0094). B put the latest game and the players side by side in two columns with a scoreboard strip, landscape player rows, and a typographic argument list. It fit more above the fold on a desktop, but it broke the single-column rhythm every other page keeps and collapsed to the same stack on a phone anyway. Top to bottom (`src/app/TeamPage.tsx` over `src/domain/teamHome.ts`):

- **The season banner** (the poster banner, ADR-0094): name-led, with the kicker "2026-27 · through Oct 28 · 5 games", the h1 "Utah Jazz", and the cue "↓ The latest game". Its art is the season's authored photograph when one is on file, the typographic poster otherwise (2025-26, and 2026-27 until the user supplies one). Under it, the seasons line and the tool note sit where a report card's way back sits.
- **01 · THE LATEST GAME**: the newest report card as a tile. The score is at poster scale over the night's title, with the two answers beside it in the card's own words, and the game's photograph is the tile's ground when it has one (credited under the tile). The three games before it follow as a row of compact tiles (date, opponent, result, the two numbers with their answer words), stacked on phones. Every number is `seasonDots`, the same function over the same rows as the card's, and every word is the card's computed copy decided on the displayed gap (ADR-0090), so a tile never disagrees with the card it opens.
- **02 · THE PLAYERS**: the featured players as portrait headshot cards, a swipe rail on phones. Each card carries his games and priced shots, his season-to-date diet and conversion against the season's frontier ruler (`buildPlayerSeason`, so a card equals his season page), the making flag below 50 priced shots, and one structural status line for his argument. Each links his season page. A featured player with no shot yet keeps a named, unlinked card reading "No shots yet", never a hidden card, because a player without a shot has no page (ADR-0095). Every other shooter is in a name-only rail below. On the living season, departed players sit under their own caption ("Not on the roster as last pulled"), since the roster is a current-state snapshot.
- **03 · THE GAMES**: the season's quadrant, every dot priced against its own day's ruler, the latest highlighted, the prior season faded behind.
- **04 · THE FLOOR**: the season's pair in the team's voice and ZONE BY ZONE, against the frontier ruler.
- **05 · THE LEDGER**: the game ledger, its rows read from the season index.
- **06 · THE ARGUMENTS**: the directory's headshot rail with the home team's arguments first, and "All arguments →" to `/arguments`.

**The status line.** It settles the plan's open detail for a featured player whose argument has not flipped. It reads "2026-27 argument published" when the hero registry carries a season argument for the page's season, and "No 2026-27 argument yet" otherwise. "Yet" is load-bearing and true: every card belongs to a living or frozen argument (the guard below), so a pending one is pending, never declined. The line flips by itself when a flip PR registers the season argument.

**The featured list is authored and guarded.** `TeamConfig.homes` carries each registered season's home config: the `featured` players in display order and the optional `banner` photograph. `src/teams/featured.test.ts` holds the featured set equal to the season's arguments, living and frozen. Living ones are `season.config.json`'s hero seasons for that season whose player is on the team's roster or has a shot for it; frozen ones are registered season arguments whose `teamPage` names the team. The config does not name a hero season's team, so that one direction reads the deployed roster and skips on a clean clone. The plan's default named only the living half. Taking the frozen half too means 2025-26 features Cody Williams, Keyonte George, and Ace Bailey instead of nothing, and the rule holds over time: Peterson's 2026-27 card stays after his flip, and the three 2026-27 cards stay after the season ends. Every featured player needs a committed headshot. The season banner, when authored, passes the game photograph's checks (`photoFieldProblems`: on disk, no em dash, the credit in a house form, both focal points), and the methodology page's SOURCES credits it like a hero banner (decision 7).

**The directory at /arguments.** Same marquee and rail (ADR-0065), ordered by `directoryOrder`: every hero with a season argument naming the home team's page first, then everyone else, each group in registry order. Registry order already puts Cody Williams, Keyonte George, and Ace Bailey first, so the move changes no figure. Its page title becomes "Arguments · Good Shots" and its hidden h1 "Arguments". Unknown paths still render the directory with its quiet note.

**The footer and the navbar.** The footer's "All players" link was the way back to the directory at `/`. It follows the directory to `/arguments` and takes its name: **Arguments**, with a four-tile glyph in place of the back arrow, since a link from the home is not a way back. It renders on every page except the directory itself, so the home now shows both utilities. The navbar's wordmark still links `/`, which is now the Jazz home.

**Share pages.** The root's emitted page and card become the Jazz's. `npm run cards:generate` draws a **season card** per registered team season (`public/social-cards/jazz/<season>.png`) in the directory card's grammar: the team's normalized mark where a headshot stands, in the banner's grayscale-inverted treatment but at figure strength, with the season as the eyebrow and "The latest game →" as the cue. The card is static, so a cached preview is true on any day and the loop never redraws it. The user chose it over pointing the root at the latest game's card, whose cached previews would go stale. ADR-0065's 2026-07-28 amendment kept share cards mark-free. That stands for every hero card; a team season's card has no headshot, so its mark is the figure, not decoration. The build re-points `dist/index.html` itself at the canonical season's meta and card with **no `og:url`** (`rootPageHtml`). The host serves that file as the fallback for every route without an emitted page (player season pages, the comparison tool), and a root `og:url` would collapse every such shared link onto the front page: index.html's original rule, kept. The root also preloads the season's files, the same four a player's season page fetches. `/arguments` gets the root's former meta and the product-wide card, and `/jazz` and `/jazz/<season>` get the season's meta. The build fails if a registered season's card is missing.

**The ledger file retires.** The season index (schema v2, ADR-0088 as amended) carries every fact a ledger row shows: the box team line and the players' lines. `aggregateTeam(payload, index)` now reads its rows there under the same oracles (the game set, box FGA at least the payload rows per game, the season box FGA equal to the payload's pre-drop total), so nothing reads `<season>.ledger.json`. `team:sync` stops copying it and both deployed files are deleted. Its producer (the loop's ledger derive and export, `ledger_facts.py`, `ledgerFacts.ts`, and the golden pair) retires in a follow-up PR, kept apart because it changes the nightly loop.

**Consequences.** A new team season joins `homes` in the registry PR that registers it, with its featured list and a season card (`npm run cards:generate`), or the suite fails. A season photograph is a registry edit plus a committed derivative and a SOURCES credit. The season card does not yet draw the photograph; teaching the card generator to do that is future work. A future contributor must not link the home's cards to the arguments directly (the player page links his argument, decision 11), must not hide a featured card, must not give the root an `og:url`, and must not compute a tile's numbers or words any way but its card's.
