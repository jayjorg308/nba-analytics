# Authored copy is drafted in the author's voice, from a committed voice guide

Authored hero copy — verdicts and their sentence families (why, line, and eventually growth) — is written in the **author's own voice**, documented as a committed voice guide (`docs/voice/VOICE.md`) distilled from red-penned before/after samples (`docs/voice/samples/`: the Befores are the pre-voice shipped 2025-26 verdicts, the Afters the author's rewrites, one pair per registered hero). The samples outrank the guide where they disagree, and every authoring session reads both before drafting hero copy (wired through CLAUDE.md's agent-skills section). Voice operates strictly **inside the house rails**: the lexicon and its claim kinds (ADR-0029), the verdict ladder (ADR-0068), the punctuation guards, and the copy-and-claims co-rewrite rule (ADR-0017) are untouched. A voice edit never re-grades a magnitude word, and a voice edit that adds a fact adds a claim.

**Why:** the five shipped verdicts were each internally honest and ladder-calibrated, but five drafting sessions had produced one recognizable house voice — formal negation ("does not"), the canonical term repeated where a fan would vary it, announcement pivots ("The creation evidence backs the bet."), seller-side value metaphors ("the priciest trips") — that read as machine-written, the exact failure the em-dash rule already named for punctuation. The 2026-07-29 red-pen sessions showed the author's voice is *learnable as rules*: the same corrections recurred independently across all five verdicts (answer word joins the first clause; concrete nouns over pronouns; announcement pivots die while judgment pivots live; comparison-class phrasing rotates; two clauses per body sentence with the payoff standing alone). Rules alone under-teach voice — cadence lives in examples — so the guide and the sample pairs ship together, and the deltas (what got struck, what replaced it) are the highest-density teaching signal.

**What the build proved about the rails:** three red-pen edits collided with data honesty, and in each case the data won before anything landed — "short mid-range jumpers at more than double" was false at the band grain (1.6x, the parent's claim), "the most valuable shots in basketball" contradicted the line-vs-floor pricing that the same verdict's closer invoked ("the cheapest points available"), and "long pull-up jumpers" asserted a pull-up × distance cross no payload carries. Voice tuning is a presentation change with a guard discipline, not a loosening: sounding like the author never licenses an unguarded claim.

**First application (this PR):** all five 2025-26 verdicts rewritten to the locked Afters, guards moved claim-for-claim — two catch-and-shoot grading words priced UP to the ladder's far bar because the data clears it (Keyonte +0.172, Ace −0.176, vs the 0.15 floor); a new within-diet share claim behind Keyonte's "the looks he gets least often"; Mitchell's assist claim flipped to the complement unassisted floor (worst-case minimum unassisted share above six in ten — 61.5% at complete coverage), and his under-bar long-two claim (1.37x league, below the bare diet-lean bar) retired with its sentence.

**Amendment (2026-07-29, same day) — first application to new heroes, and the corpus's second pair kind.** Adds six and seven (Nique Clifford, Maxime Raynaud) were the guide's first live test: verdicts drafted under it landed close enough that the author's red-pens were light and voice-consistent (a rhythm split, plainer verbs, natural basketball phrasing over zone-precise phrasing). Both pairs were archived with Before = the voice-guided draft and After = the red-pen, so the corpus now teaches the guide's *residual* error alongside the founding five's house-to-author delta — the feedback loop the workflow section always intended, running for real. Two durable additions came out of the red-pens and are recorded in the guide: the ledger metaphor family ("breaks even", "the shortfall") joined the buyer's-side value language, and the **watch sentence** — a hedged forward-looking fragment naming a flagged signal ("Worth watching whether the right corner holds up on real volume") — entered the repertoire with a matching guard shape: the claim asserts the warm bin AND the small-sample flag's presence, so the hedge is honest by assertion rather than by tone. The rails held here too: the same red-pens produced two data corrections ("grades out well" at a rim that reads neutral became "breaks even"; "the only place" gained its "about" for the equally neutral above-the-break zone), each caught against the payloads before landing.

**Consequences:** the guide's vocabulary calls bind future copy — the cost noun is "value", never "efficiency" (readers map efficiency to eFG%/TS%, the framings ADR-0001 rejects); "shot making" stays unhyphenated; absolute frequency words yield to true comparatives when the raw share would surprise a reader who then meets the table; scope words are checked against the metrics before being trimmed or widened. Red-pen deltas feed the guide: when the author's edit contradicts a rule, the rule changes and the correction is recorded. A future contributor must not treat the guide as a generator — verdicts stay authored, never computed (ADR-0017/0063's boundary, unmoved) — and must not "align" a frozen season's verdict to a later voice revision: like every frozen argument, shipped copy changes only when its own data or author moves it.
