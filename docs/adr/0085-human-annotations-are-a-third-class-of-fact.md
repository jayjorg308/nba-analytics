# Human annotations are a third class of fact

The foul record introduces **foul annotations**: assertions a named reviewer makes about one **foul event** after watching it (court zone and click location, ball status, play type, who fed the fouled player, defenders within reach, a short note), stored in the record store's `foul_annotation` table under a **versioned vocabulary** and a written **labeling guide**. This is a third class of fact beside the two the product already has. It is not **sourced** (observed by the NBA and reconciled to an oracle), and it is not **authored copy** (judgment about what the data means, guarded by claims). It is an **observation made by a human**, and the product treats it as such: recorded with provenance (reviewer, time, vocabulary version), with **unknown** as a first-class value in every field, and never rendered beside sourced numbers without that provenance stated.

**Why a table apart, and why a view feeds it.** The foul event itself is sourced: every Foul action in a Jazz game, with period, clock, fouler, foul type, referee, penalty state, and the fouled player where the record names him through the same-clock free throws or the same-clock made shot. That is a derivation over `pbp_event` and lives as a **view** (`foul_event`), never a table, per ADR-0080's rule that the store stores observations and computes derivations. The annotation table holds only what no source observed, keyed to the event by game and action number (ADR-0036's exact identity), so the sourced half can be rebuilt from raw at any time without touching a single human assertion, and the human half can be audited without wondering which of its columns the NBA said.

**Honesty rules for this class.** The zone is **human-picked** from the six evaluation zones, with the court click stored as the fine-grain supplement, because no NBA zone exists for a foul and the product has never assigned zones geometrically (ADR-0012); a later geometric classifier may grade the picks, never replace them. A **game is reviewed** only when every foul drawn in it is annotated or explicitly unknown, the taxonomy-totality pattern of ADR-0053 applied to review. The vocabulary changes only by migration with a version bump, and rows never rewrite under a new version. Reviewer drift is measured, not assumed away: a random sample is re-reviewed mid-season against the guide.

**Consequences.** Annotations accumulate from the first reviewed game and have **no public surface in the Jazz surface plan**; their first rendering is a later decision with its own honesty design, and any such surface must label the class plainly, keep it visually apart from reconciled numbers, and show the reviewed-game denominator. The review tool is local-only and writes only to `foul_annotation`. The record store stays product-blind: the table knows games and players, never heroes or slugs. A future contributor must not backfill an unknown from a model, must not merge annotation columns into a sourced table, and must not compute a league comparison from annotations.
