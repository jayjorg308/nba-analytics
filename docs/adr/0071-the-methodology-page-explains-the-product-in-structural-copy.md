# The methodology page explains the product in structural copy

The site gains its one self-explanation surface: **`/methodology`**, a static route of structural product copy explaining the model (expected value, PPS, the league-diet benchmark and its stated comparison class, the two-axis decomposition), the honesty rules (the inclusion bar, the † flag, dropped-and-counted exclusions, the frontier byline), the data story (official sources, exact reconciliation, committed payloads), the authored-verdict-kept-honest-by-tests stance, a **vocabulary** section, and a closing **SOURCES** section crediting the site's imagery. The directory gains its companion: a one-paragraph **blurb** between the marquee and the "More verdicts" rail, ending in a plain link here — closing ROADMAP launch items 8 and 2 together, exactly as item 8 anticipated ("the low-footprint answer to item 2"). The shared footer carries a **Methodology** link on every page. The page's copy is structural only: no per-hero claims, no per-hero numbers, so nothing on it carries verdict-guard obligations.

**Why a page and a blurb, not a deck:** the marquee redesign (ADR-0065) deliberately dropped the index's explanatory deck, and item 2 recorded the tension — a cold visitor now meets a face and a cue with no statement of what the site is. Restoring a deck would spend the marquee's restraint on copy a returning reader rereads forever; prominence follows differentiation (ADR-0018), and what differentiates the tool is the argued verdict, not a pitch. The blurb is the smallest honest answer (two sentences and a link, under the marquee, above the rail — no heading, so the outline stays site → featured name → rail), and the page is where the full story lives for exactly the reader who wants it.

**The vocabulary section re-renders the glossary registry.** `src/app/glossary.ts` is already the product's one dictionary (ADR-0052); the page prints every entry as a definition list by iterating the registry, so a new term appears here automatically and this page can never disagree with a popover. Definitions print in place, which is also why the page carries **no Term popovers**: popping a definition over the page that already prints it is circular. The page test locks the page button-free (the index's own no-switcher discipline) — a deliberate lock, relaxed only on purpose.

**`methodology` is a reserved route.** The router stays route-table-free (`parseRoute` is untouched); `App.tsx` resolves the reserved slug before the registry lookup, and `RESERVED_ROUTES` (`src/app/routes.ts`) is asserted against every hero slug in the registry coherence tests — a future `hero:add` for a player whose slug collides with a static page fails loudly instead of silently losing its page.

**The footer link, and never-links-to-itself.** `SiteFooter` gains `methodologyLink`, default **true**, so every page carries the way to the page without touching call sites; the methodology page itself opts out. Both sides are guarded: the index's chrome-link count covers its two methodology links (blurb + footer), and the page test asserts no anchor on `/methodology` resolves to `/methodology`.

**The tab title is the second exception.** `document.title` is `Methodology · Good Shots` — CONTEXT.md's bare-wordmark tab rule gains its second exception on the hero pages' own rationale (a reader with several tabs open needs to tell them apart), keeping the wordmark because, unlike a player's name, a bare "Methodology" identifies nothing. Still a label, never a sentence.

**Image credits become product copy, with an honest pending state.** `HeroBannerConfig` gains optional `imageCredit`: the reader-facing credit line SOURCES renders per registered hero, in registry order. Absent means genuinely unknown and renders "photograph credit pending" — the dropped-and-counted ethos applied to provenance: stated plainly, never blank, never guessed. The authoring tripwire scans the field for scaffold sentinels but never reds on absence (five shipped banners' credits are pending research; holding the suite red on them would block every unrelated change), and the scaffolder deliberately does not emit the field — the credit stays named human work on `hero:add`'s closing checklist (ADR-0066). Mechanical assets (headshots, team marks, the wordmark, the generated share cards) are covered by blanket lines. `docs/image-credits.md` remains the engineering provenance record (URLs, pull dates); the two update together.

**Build emission: ADR-0067's mechanism, first non-hero use.** `npm run build` emits `dist/methodology/index.html` through the same `heroPageHtml` transform over the same font-preload-injected source, from hand-built meta (`methodologyPageMeta`): swapped title and description, its own `og:url`, zero payload preloads (the page fetches nothing), and the **product-wide card retained** — the page is the product explaining itself, not a hero, so it shares the root's card rather than earning a generated one.

**Amendment (2026-07-30, same day — the footer re-forms into three registered zones, and the blurb's link becomes a cue).** The first build's footer put four items of very different visual weight in one space-between row (way-back pill, tagline, a text methodology link, the social rings), so the middle floated unevenly and the tagline changed position between page classes — the author called it out on sight. The footer is now a **1fr auto 1fr grid**: utility rings left (the way back as a left-arrow icon, the methodology page as an open-book icon — the site's one reading surface, saying so; chosen over the info "i" and a question mark in a three-way comparison on the live footer, the "?" reading as help once a label sat beside it), the tagline dead-center on every page, the social rings right. One ring grammar carries both ends (`.footer-ring` shares the social row's 44px recipe); the way-back pill is retired, and accessible names ride aria-labels ("All players", "Methodology") exactly as the social icons' always have. On phones the tagline signs off on top with both ring groups in one centered row beneath. The blurb's link moved onto its own line in the marquee cue's register (caps, tracked, with the arrow: "How the argument works →") — the site's established grammar for "what happens next", and a phrase that can no longer wrap mid-link. One adaptive rule completes it: a utility that stands **alone** in its zone spells itself out as a labeled pill (the ring recipe stretched — icon plus text, same border, height, and colors), while utilities in company compress to icon rings. So hero pages show the compact `← ?` pair, the index's lone methodology link reads "Methodology" in full, and the methodology page's lone way back reads "← All players" — the two pages where a single unlabeled glyph would have floated are exactly the two where the label matters most (the cold visitor's index, the shared-link reader's methodology page). Nothing this ADR decided moved: both surfaces still exist, the page still never links itself, and the chrome-link count still guards the index side.

**Amendment (2026-07-30, same day) — structural copy gets a claim-by-claim verification pass, because it makes checkable claims.** The page's first draft read cleanly and was wrong in six places. The author's red-pen pass, run section by section against the constants, the aggregation, and the deployed payloads, found: "The two add up exactly" introducing a three-term identity (the league's diet is the anchor, not a third axis); "exactly on the points per shot he scored" silently excluding backcourt heaves (ADR-0016) and dropped zone-conflict rows (ADR-0019), and readable as folding in free throws; "a zone enters the argument at 15 attempts" contradicting `aggregate.ts`, which weights the diet over all six zones regardless of `included` and was falsifiable on two live pages (SGA's corners at 13 and 5, Raynaud's at 14 and 11); "the sources must agree with each other exactly" contradicting its own next paragraph and false twice over (the league tracking universe runs ~0.37% under official FGA by design, and Ace Bailey carries a pinned 8-attempt shortfall); the verdict ladder's **floor** sold as a fixed price ("promises the same magnitude" where Cody's declared `FAR_BELOW_PPS = 0.25` outweighs the ladder's 0.15); and "the verdicts are written by a person, never generated", which was untrue and which ADR-0070's own sample corpus contradicts.

**What this establishes.** Structural copy carries no verdict-guard obligations (this ADR's own rule, unchanged) — and that is exactly why it needs the pass: nothing fails when it drifts, so a false sentence ships reading perfectly and stays shipped. Two working rules came out of it. First, **verify against the code and the payloads, never against the description of them**: every one of the six survived a reading for tone and died on contact with a constant or a count. Second, **a claim true of today's roster is not a true claim** — the heave exclusion is invisible while no hero has taken one, and the fifteen-attempt error was invisible until a hero shot thin corners. Where a phrase is load-bearing (the scoping in "from the field, over every attempt the model counts", the "at least" in the ladder sentence), the source comment says so, because the next editor will read those qualifiers as clutter. The disclosure that replaced the authorship boast is the pattern's sharpest case: the honest claim (a judgment a person signs off on, held to the data by tests) is both true and stronger than the one it replaced.

**Consequences:** a new glossary entry ships on this page for free; registering a hero publishes its credit line (or its pending line) here for free; filling a pending credit is a one-field config change plus an image-credits.md note. A future contributor must not add per-hero claims or numbers to this page (structural copy is the contract), must not reintroduce a deck on the marquee (fix this page or the blurb instead), must not give the page a Term popover without revisiting the button-free lock, and must not let a static route ship without joining `RESERVED_ROUTES`.
