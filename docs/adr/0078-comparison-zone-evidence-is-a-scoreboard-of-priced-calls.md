# Comparison zone evidence is a scoreboard of priced calls

The comparison page's zone evidence renders as six per-zone cards rather than one combined table: the plan's twelve-column accessible twin asked the reader to track four metric groups across paired side columns, and the per-zone question ("who has what here?") had no surface of its own. Each card leads with two calls over a compact stat table of both windows plus the league ruler row (share and FG% — so every number reads against its baseline in place, and each making Δ renders beside its displayed anchors, the ZoneDetailCard discipline). Diet lean names the window taking the larger displayed attempt share, priced in share points; Making edge names the window with the higher displayed making Δ, priced in FG percentage points; margins render unitless beside the anchors they subtract, with the notes naming the units. A call decides and prices on the gap of its two displayed anchors (ADR-0023), so the chip and the numbers beneath it always subtract; margins under 1.0 display units read as even, because a sub-point gap between displayed numbers is not evidence of a claim; a call inherits † whenever either window carries the corresponding flag (ADR-0075). The vocabulary is deliberately asymmetric: "edge" is claimed only on the making axis, where higher is a result against the league, while the diet axis says "leans" because selection is a preference. No call grades the comparison as a whole; the page stays a tool, not an argument. Chosen from a four-variant throwaway prototype against the shipping table (combined baseline, split diet/making tables, bar panels, per-zone scoreboard).
