# Computed copy is authored templates behind band predicates

The team report card reads a direction into its numbers ("No, below average"), which ADR-0081 forbids a tool-class surface to do in structural copy. Computed copy is the fourth class of copy beside structural copy, authored verdicts, and human annotations (ADR-0085): sentences authored once as templates, filled mechanically, each emitted only when the predicate that licenses it holds. It amends ADR-0081 for the team surface, and only through templates. The templates live in one registry, `src/teams/computedCopy.ts`, under their own guard (`computedCopy.test.ts`): the house punctuation (no em dash, colon, or semicolon), no grade or causal vocabulary (no letter grades, no "because", "defense", "luck", "effort", "should"), the bands partitioning the line, and, over every deployed game, the words a card shows are exactly the words its displayed gap's band licenses. A template that fails the guard is restructured; a guard list is never weakened.

**The bands are the verdict ladder's, measured at game grain.** Decided 2026-09-28 from the 2025-26 distribution (`docs/research/pre-game-ruler-2025-26.md`). The "Good shots?" answer uses the season ladder unchanged (neutral inside 0.02, a bit to 0.05, bare to 0.10, well to 0.15, far beyond), which at game grain spread the 82 games 30 neutral, 37 a bit, 15 bare, none well or far. The "Did they fall?" answer uses twice its bars (0.04 / 0.10 / 0.20 / 0.30), because game-grain making swings far wider: on the season ladder 40 of 82 games read well or far, at twice the bars 12 do. The scaled bars only ever make a card's word stricter than the ladder's floor, never looser (ADR-0068).

**The words are the ladder's own, authored 2026-10-22.** Drafted from `docs/voice/VOICE.md` and its samples, then red-penned by the author. The prototype's placeholders priced magnitude off the ladder ("Slightly", "Very", "really", "Great", "Bad") and said two untrue things: "Nothing fell" for a far-below night on which the Jazz still made shots, and a bare title ("tough shots that fell") beside a hedged answer on 27 of 82 cards. The authored set:

- **The answers open the way a verdict does.** Inside the material bar the hedge is the answer ("A bit below average"); past it, "Yes" or "No" leads ("No, below average", "Yes, well above expectation"). The two answers rotate their comparison class, "average" for the diet and "expectation" for the making, so a card never says the same phrase twice.
- **The night's title keeps the hedge.** Each side has five states (above, a bit above, league average, a bit below, below), so "a bit" in an answer is never dropped from the title beside it; bare, well, and far all title as the bare comparative, which understates and stays inside the ladder ("a bit below-average shots that trailed expectation by a bit", "league-average shots that beat expectation"). The cost is length: the longest title wraps to four lines at 375px, five of 2025-26's games.
- **The chart's corners stay conversational.** A corner names a region of the chart, never a game, so it carries sign only and no band. The draft named regions with the title's precise phrasing and the author's red pen restored the short form: "good shots that fell", "costly shots that didn't fall". "Costly" replaced the prototype's "tough" on purpose: the axis prices a diet, it never measures how hard a shot was (difficulty belongs to the creation family's defender distance, which needs its own claim kind), and "costly" is the value ledger's word from the verdicts ("a diet that costs him real value").
- **Ranks place a game, never grade it.** Counted from the nearer end: "most valuable diet", "29th-least valuable diet", "3rd-furthest above expectation", never best and worst, and absent below ten games (`SEASON_RANK_MIN_GAMES`).

**Consequences.** `AUTHORED` in the registry is true from 2026-10-22, so the page no longer tags computed sentences as draft. The answer words are drawn into every game's share card, so any change to them reruns `npm run cards:games` in the same PR. A change to a template rewrites its guard expectations in the same PR; the guard's forbidden lists only grow. Season superlatives and player notes (the plan's later templates) join this registry by amendment, scoped by their own words ("this season", "Jazz games") until league context exists.
