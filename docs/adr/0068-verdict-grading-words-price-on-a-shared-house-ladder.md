# Verdict grading words price on a shared house ladder

The magnitude words in authored verdicts — "essentially league average", "a little", a bare "above", "well above", "far above" — now price on one **house ladder** (`src/heroes/verdictLadder.ts`), consumed by every season argument's guard and by `hero:report`'s CLAIM HEADROOM header. The same word promises at least the same magnitude on every page: a reader who calibrates "well above" on one hero's argument carries that calibration to the next, so the words are a cross-page currency, and a currency needs one mint.

**What forced it.** The 2026-07-28 verdict consistency pass (ROADMAP launch item 5) audited all five guards' thresholds and found the words drifting apart in exactly the way five independent authoring sessions would produce: "far" was priced at 0.15 PPS on Shai's page and 0.25 on Cody's; Ace's bare "convert above league value" cleared at the neutral edge (0.02) while Mitchell's equally bare "beat the league pull-up value" required material (0.05); and one constant was named `MATERIAL_MAKING_PPS` while carrying the neutral-edge value. Each guard was internally honest — ADR-0017's discipline held — but the roster's vocabulary was not one language.

**The ladder.** For PPS gaps (selection Δ, making Δ, creation PPS vs league): inside ±0.02 is "essentially league average"; past neutral but short of 0.05 must be hedged ("a little", "a bit"); ≥0.05 licenses a bare comparative; ≥0.10 "well above/below"; ≥0.15 "far above/below". Analog families: diet leans (0.05 share points bare, 0.10 "far more than is typical"), free-throw conversion ("well" = 5 FT% points, both technical cuts per ADR-0055), FTA rate (0.02 floor for any directional claim, "far" from 0.10, "a bit" two-sided between them). Approximation words ("nearly N", "about N", "roughly N", "close to N") are **two-sided bands** wherever they appear — the phrase overstates below its band and understates above it (the Mitchell guard's stated rationale, promoted house-wide; "more/fewer than N" stays one-sided by construction).

**Floors, not exact prices.** A guard assigns its claim-named constant from the ladder, or declares a documented **stricter** local bar — Cody's `FAR_BELOW_PPS = 0.25` stands, because his catch-and-shoot gap (−0.390) supports pricing "far below" at five times materiality — never a looser one. This is the relationship ADR-0029's lexicon already has to vocabulary, extended from *which words are legal* to *what the words weigh*. ADR-0017/0059's stance stands: guards stay exact, declare their own claim mappings with rationale, and never consume the headroom report; what changes is that the recurring bars now have one definition instead of five hand-copies.

**Applied at adoption:** Ace's bare-comparative bar rose 0.02 → 0.05 (actual +0.051 — a 0.001 margin, acceptable only on a frozen season; a living season would rewrite the sentence instead); Shai's "far" constants now read from the ladder's 0.15; Keyonte's misnamed constant became `POSITIVE_MAKING_PPS = NEUTRAL_BAND_PPS` (the claim is direction, not magnitude); Keyonte's "about half" gained its floor (0.4x) and Shai's and Ace's "nearly triple" their ceilings (3x), all passing with real margin. CLAIM HEADROOM grew the "far" tier so the authoring ruler names every rung.

**What must not regress:** never loosen a guard to the ladder (stricter local bars are declarations, not drift); never add a rung by picking a number in one guard (a new grading word enters the ladder first, with rationale); and the ladder prices *house words* only — claim-specific quantities ("almost all", "one in five", "roughly a quarter", "MVP-level") stay locally declared with their own rationale, though they may borrow a ladder value when it genuinely fits.
