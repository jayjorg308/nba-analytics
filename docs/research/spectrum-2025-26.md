# The 2025-26 selection and making spectrum

Every player at 300+ priced field-goal attempts (284 qualifiers), decomposed the way
the site prices a season: **expected PPS from his diet** (his attempts priced at the
league's zone rates — what his choices were worth) against **actual PPS** on the same
attempts, their difference the conversion above or below his own diet. Backcourt heaves
and zone-point conflicts are excluded from pricing (ADR-0019); computed 2026-08-26 from
the record store. League diet PPS: **1.091**.

Selection is priced, conversion is what happened over one season — a ranking, not an
ability estimate.
